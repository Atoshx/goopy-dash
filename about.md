# Goopy Dash

Goopy Le Grande, the first boss of **Cuphead**, bounces into your Geometry Dash levels.
He hops along ahead of you, slams down to send slime dust rolling at your feet,
and stretches out his boxing glove to punch. Jump over it all and reach the end of the level to beat him.

Goopy's art and sounds are read from **your own Cuphead install** while you play; nothing from Cuphead is included in this mod.
Requires Geometry Dash 2.2081 and Cuphead on PC.
