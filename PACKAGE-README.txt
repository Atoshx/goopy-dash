Goopy Dash for Geometry Dash 2.2081
===================================
Goopy Le Grande, Cuphead's first boss, chases you through Geometry Dash levels.
He hops along ahead of you, slams down to send slime dust rolling at your feet,
and stretches out his boxing glove to punch. Reach the end of a level to beat him.

Needs Cuphead (Steam). Goopy's art and sounds are read from your own Cuphead
install while you play; nothing from Cuphead is included here.

Installed by Melty into your Geometry Dash folder:
  Geode.dll, XInput1_4.dll, GeodeUpdater.exe  - the Geode mod loader (Geode Team, BSL-1.0)
  geode/mods/dlg.goopy-dash.geode             - the Goopy Dash mod (DLG, MIT)

Settings: in Geometry Dash open Geode's mod list, pick Goopy Dash and press the
gear to make Goopy harmless, mute him, or change how often he attacks.
Source: https://github.com/Atoshx/goopy-dash
